# Dragonbound — Hero Dragon Concept Brief (M2)

> Status: M2 production brief  
> Purpose: source-of-truth art direction for the juvenile hero dragon and future growth stages.

## 1. Core silhouette

The dragon should read immediately as a **lean, elegant flying predator**, not a bulky fantasy lizard.

- Long, slightly serpentine neck with visible functional musculature.
- Narrow, expressive head and strong jaw silhouette.
- Large swept membrane wings; wing fingers and leading edge must remain readable.
- Long, flexible tail that acts as both a flight stabilizer and an emotional body-language tool.
- Athletic quadruped torso and limbs: powerful enough to feel believable, light enough to feel agile.
- Layered scales with larger structural scales over the chest/neck and finer scales over limbs and face.
- Secondary filaments/crest structures may frame the head, neck, and tail, but must remain original and restrained.

## 2. Juvenile target — M2

M2 needs the **juvenile companion**, not the final adult hero.

The juvenile should:
- be clearly capable of walking/running beside the Rider;
- have wings large enough to establish the future flight silhouette, but remain proportionally young;
- have slightly oversized eyes/head features for readable emotion without becoming cartoonish;
- retain the long-tail identity;
- look physically young through proportions, scale development, horn/crest development, and musculature rather than simply being an adult model scaled down;
- feel agile, curious, and slightly awkward in movement.

Do not build flight gameplay yet. The wings are part of the character identity and future animation requirements, not an M2 flight mechanic.

## 3. Face and emotional readability

The dragon's face is a gameplay interface.

Required readable signals:
- eye direction and blink timing;
- ear/crest/head movement where anatomy supports it;
- jaw tension and mouth opening;
- neck posture;
- tail posture;
- wing fold/open tension;
- breathing and small idle movements.

The emotional system should be readable before the player sees any UI explanation.

## 4. Material direction

Target: realistic cinematic creature materials.

- Fine scale breakup visible in close-up.
- Slight variation between structural scales and softer underside plates.
- Membranes should have believable translucency/subsurface response.
- Dirt, wear, moisture, and environmental contact should accumulate naturally.
- Avoid plastic-looking uniform roughness.
- Elemental effects are not baked into the base creature material; Ember comes later as a layered state.

## 5. Color direction

Final palette is **TBD**.

Do not lock the dragon to the grayscale reference. The reference establishes anatomy, silhouette, wing language, and mood only.

The final palette should:
- remain believable under natural lighting;
- provide enough contrast around the eyes and facial planes for emotion;
- support Ember later without becoming permanently neon or red;
- work in daylight, dusk, firelight, and fog.

## 6. Growth continuity

Hatchling → Juvenile → Young Adult → Adult must feel like the same individual.

Continuity should come from:
- recurring scale-pattern motifs;
- eye identity;
- recognizable facial structure;
- tail/crest language;
- palette family;
- distinctive markings.

Each stage gets a designed silhouette rather than a uniform scale multiplier.

## 7. Originality rule

The supplied visual reference is an **anatomical and mood reference only**.

Do not trace, reproduce, retopologize, texture, or otherwise derive the final Dragonbound asset from a copyrighted creature design. Final anatomy, facial design, markings, proportions, palette, crest/filament arrangement, and materials must be authored as original Dragonbound work.

## 8. 3D production target

Preferred hero pipeline:
1. original concept/model design;
2. high-detail sculpt;
3. game-ready retopology;
4. UVs and hero material sets;
5. skeletal rig with wing, tail, neck, jaw, eye and facial controls;
6. juvenile locomotion and emotional animation set;
7. Unreal skeletal mesh + Animation Blueprint;
8. LOD/scalability pass for the phone-first target.

### M2 minimum asset set

- Juvenile skeletal mesh.
- Basic material.
- Skeleton/rig.
- Idle.
- Walk.
- Run.
- Turn.
- Sit/rest or grounded idle.
- Wing fold/unfold.
- Simple reaction poses for curious, comforted, distressed, and protective states.

No flight animation set is required until M6.
